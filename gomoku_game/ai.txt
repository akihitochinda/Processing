How did you determine which piece to play next? Tell us about your "pick next move"
algorithm
-> The algorithm prioritizes to check for any immediate winning moves for both itself and the
   opponent. Then it evaluates potential moves based on a system that considers factors like
   searching open-ended lines or enough space to build towards a win meanwhile blocking opponent's
   threats. (e.g., prioritizing movements in order 5 -> 4 -> 3 -> 2 stone consequtives, avoiding
   edge cases or dead ends like dead 4s or 3s with no open ends)

How easy would it be to make the difficulty level adjustable?
